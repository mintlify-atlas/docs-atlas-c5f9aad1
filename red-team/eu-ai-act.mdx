The EU Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act), enacted in 2024, is the world's first comprehensive legal framework for AI systems. It uses a risk-based approach: outright prohibiting certain practices, heavily regulating high-risk systems, and providing lighter requirements for limited- and minimal-risk AI. Promptfoo's red teaming capabilities cover both **Article 5 prohibited practices** and **Annex III high-risk system requirements**.

## Running an EU AI Act scan

Use the `eu:ai-act` shorthand to test across all covered categories at once:

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act
  strategies:
    - jailbreak:meta
    - jailbreak:composite
    - jailbreak-templates
```

Target specific articles or risk categories directly:

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act:art5:subliminal-manipulation
    - eu:ai-act:annex3:biometric-id
    - eu:ai-act:annex3:law-enforcement
```

## Article 5: Prohibited practices

### Subliminal manipulation (`eu:ai-act:art5:subliminal-manipulation`)

AI must not deploy subliminal techniques to materially distort user behavior in a way that causes harm.

**Promptfoo coverage:** `hijacking`, `intent`, `excessive-agency`.

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act:art5:subliminal-manipulation
    # Equivalent to:
    - hijacking
    - intent
    - excessive-agency
  strategies:
    - jailbreak:meta
    - jailbreak:composite
    - jailbreak-templates
```

### Exploitation of vulnerabilities (`eu:ai-act:art5:exploitation-of-vulnerabilities`)

AI must not exploit the age, disability, or social/economic situation of persons to distort their behavior harmfully.

**Promptfoo coverage:** `hijacking`, `imitation`, `harmful:misinformation-disinformation`.

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act:art5:exploitation-of-vulnerabilities
    # Equivalent to:
    - hijacking
    - imitation
    - harmful:misinformation-disinformation
```

### Social scoring (`eu:ai-act:art5:social-scoring`)

AI must not evaluate or classify persons based on social behavior in ways that cause detrimental treatment.

**Promptfoo coverage:** `harmful:misinformation-disinformation`, `overreliance`.

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act:art5:social-scoring
    # Equivalent to:
    - harmful:misinformation-disinformation
    - overreliance
```

### Biometric categorisation (`eu:ai-act:art5:biometric-categorisation`)

AI must not infer race, political opinions, religious beliefs, sex life, or sexual orientation from biometric data.

**Promptfoo coverage:** `pii:direct`, `pii:session`, `harmful:privacy`.

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act:art5:biometric-categorisation
    # Equivalent to:
    - pii:direct
    - pii:session
    - harmful:privacy
```

### Remote biometric identification (`eu:ai-act:art5:remote-biometric-id-live`, `eu:ai-act:art5:remote-biometric-id-post`)

Real-time and post-event remote biometric identification in public spaces for law enforcement are prohibited with narrow exceptions.

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act:art5:remote-biometric-id-live
    - eu:ai-act:art5:remote-biometric-id-post
```

## Bias testing

Bias is a central concern for high-risk AI under the EU AI Act. Use the `harmful:hate`, `harmful:harassment-bullying`, and `harmful:insults` plugins, or target the employment and education Annex III categories which include bias-related coverage:

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - harmful:hate
    - harmful:harassment-bullying
    - harmful:insults
    - eu:ai-act:annex3:employment  # Bias in hiring decisions
    - eu:ai-act:annex3:education   # Bias in educational assessment
```

## Transparency requirements

The EU AI Act requires AI systems to inform users they are interacting with AI, mark AI-generated content, and support detection of deepfakes. Test whether your system resists attempts to impersonate humans or obscure its AI nature:

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - imitation       # Does the system impersonate a human?
    - excessive-agency  # Does it act without user awareness?
```

## Combining with other frameworks

```yaml
redteam:
  plugins:
    - eu:ai-act
    - nist:ai:measure
    - owasp:llm
  strategies:
    - jailbreak:meta
    - jailbreak:composite
    - jailbreak-templates
```

<Note>
Automated red teaming addresses technical risk identification. Full EU AI Act compliance also requires documentation (risk management system, technical docs, instructions for use), transparency obligations (AI disclosure, content marking), human oversight mechanisms, and post-market monitoring. Violations of prohibited practices carry fines up to €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover.
</Note>
